About AUI Sign

AUI Sign is a modern attendance management system designed specifically for Augustine University students. Our platform streamlines the attendance process, making it quick, reliable, and environmentally friendly. With AUI Sign, you can sign your attendance digitally, view your attendance history, and stay organized throughout the semester.
